# Weather illustration prompts

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. The approved clear-day illustration is the shared visual reference. Assets are WebP conversions of the generated PNGs. Clear and partly cloudy have separate moonlit artwork; other scenes use a CSS nighttime tint.

## Shared edit prompt

Use case: lighting-weather. Edit the reference into a weather-app background variant. Preserve exactly its wide 16:9 composition, three rolling hill contours in the bottom third, three cypress trees at far right, handmade cut-paper illustration style, slightly imperfect edges and subtle paper texture. Upper center must remain quiet for UI. Limited palette of 6 flat colors, no gradients, no 3D, no glow, no text, no labels, no UI, no frame. 

## Variants

### clear-night

Clear night. Replace sun with a small cream crescent moon and just five tiny sparse stars. Ink blue sky, desaturated blue-green hills.

### partly-cloudy

Partly cloudy day. Keep the sun but partially cover it with two large simple cream paper clouds. Keep the blue sky and green ochre hills.

### partly-cloudy-night

Partly cloudy night. Replace sun with cream crescent moon partly covered by two broad slate-blue paper clouds. Ink-blue sky, blue-green hills. Three tiny stars.

### overcast

Fully overcast. Remove sun entirely. Broad overlapping gray-blue cut-paper cloud banks along upper edge. Muted gray-sage hills. Quiet gray-blue sky.

### fog

Dense mist and fog. Remove sun. Pale gray sky. Broad translucent-looking but flat paper bands of pale gray across hills, trees partially obscured. Subdued sage hills.

### drizzle

Light drizzle. Remove sun. A broad soft slate cloud bank along the upper edge. Very few delicate short fine diagonal rain marks mostly near edges. Muted sage and teal hills, gray-blue sky.

### rain

Steady rain. Remove sun. Large slate-blue cloud bank on upper edge. Sparse deliberate diagonal pale rain strokes. Dark teal and sage hills, slate sky.

### storm

Thunderstorm. Remove sun. Heavy deep slate cloud bank along top. One small angular butter-cream lightning bolt near upper right, a few diagonal rain marks. Deep desaturated teal hills. Dramatic but flat and restrained.

### snow

Gentle snow. Remove sun. Pale blue-gray sky, cream snow covering the same rolling hill contours with blue-gray undersides, same three trees dark and lightly snow-covered. Sparse small irregular white paper dots.

### blizzard

Blizzard and blowing snow. Remove sun. Snow-covered cream hills and blue-gray sky. Broad sweeping diagonal pale wind ribbons and sparse snow marks, partially obscured trees. Still very minimal.

### ice

Sleet and freezing precipitation. Remove sun. Slate sky and broad cloud bank, frosty muted teal hills edged with cream frost. Sparse mixed short rain strokes and tiny cream ice-pellet diamonds.

### haze

Haze, dust or smoke. Remove sun disc entirely. Muted warm gray and dusty ochre sky, hills faded into overlapping taupe and sage layers, hazy horizontal bands. No flames or disaster imagery.


## Clear-day reference

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a finished landscape illustration as a new background direction for a weather app. Wide 16:9 composition. Art direction: charming restrained mid-century cut-paper editorial illustration, confident slightly imperfect hand-cut contours, flat ink colors with extremely subtle paper tooth. A simple rural hillside landscape: three broad asymmetrical rolling hill shapes in muted sage, deep forest green and dusty ochre, one small cluster of three dark cypress silhouettes at the far right, one pale butter-yellow sun in a dusty blue sky. Horizon low, bottom 35 percent contains landscape, upper 65 percent is spacious quiet sky for weather UI overlay. Sophisticated composition, visually memorable through shape and proportion, just 6 colors, no gradients, no 3D, no glow, no intricate detail, no photorealistic mountains, no fantasy, no generic AI wallpaper, no text, no frame, no UI, no watermark. Edge-to-edge artwork. Make the landscape feel human-designed and subtly playful, not sterile geometric stock art.
